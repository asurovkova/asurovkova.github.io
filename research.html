// Research

Exploring how people learn, create, and perceive.

I explore how interactive technologies can help creatives and makers deepen their understanding of their craft, develop their skills, and discover new possibilities through hands-on experimentation.

I also study how sensory substitution and spatial audio can support awareness of our surroundings. My work combines user research, co-design, prototyping, and evaluation across creative practice, Extended Reality, and accessible interaction.

Core question
How can interactive technologies support learning, creative exploration, and awareness of the world around us?
Research interests
Creative Practice // Skill Learning // Sensory Substitution // Extended Reality // Accessibility
Outcome
Lasting skills, creative exploration, and accessible interaction

Research Directions

Creative Practice & Skill Learning

Interactive feedback that helps makers understand their craft, explore new possibilities, and retain skills beyond the learning session.

Sensory Substitution & Multimodal Interaction

Translating information about the surrounding environment into sensory cues, including spatial audio, that people can understand and act on.

XR & Accessible Interaction

Co-designing spatial interfaces that connect digital information with everyday activities and support diverse ways of perceiving and communicating.
